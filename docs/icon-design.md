# 图标设计

本版使用内置图像生成工具生成，随后仅转换尺寸及 ICO 格式。

应用资源：`public/app-icon.png`；Windows 图标：`src-tauri/icons/icon.ico`。

设计方向：暖珊瑚色底、米白纸页、平面几何形状，在小尺寸下仍清晰。

生成提示词：

> Design one polished flat app icon for Interleaf, a personal paper reading and notes desktop app. A restrained editorial identity, extremely simple and memorable at 32 px. Center a single bold ivory emblem of an open book made of two clean geometric paper leaves, separated by a narrow central negative-space gap. The leaves have gently rounded outer corners and a subtle stepped page silhouette, no page lines. Solid muted warm coral terracotta rounded-square tile (#DF806C), ivory mark (#FFFAF2), only these two flat colors. Precise balanced proportions, generous breathing room, emblem occupies about 58 percent of the tile. Crisp flat vector-like graphic, clean uniform silhouette. No letters, no words, no PDF badges, no pencil, no bookmarks, no realistic paper, no curled sheets, no 3D, no gradients, no gloss, no bevel, no glow, no texture, no shadows, no mockup, no scene. Single square icon centered and fills 90 percent of the image; genuinely transparent outside the rounded-square tile, no stray pixels or decorative fragments.
